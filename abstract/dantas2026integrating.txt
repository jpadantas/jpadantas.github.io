This work presents a research framework that brings together detailed simulation and machine learning to improve decision support and autonomy for aerial vehicles, with beyond-visual-range (BVR) air combat as a specific case study in the air domain. The framework covers five connected areas: a simulation environment and services; learning-based models of engagement zones and kill probability; in-flight tactical decision support for launch timing and situational awareness; tools for aerospace data analysis; and autonomous agents trained through reinforcement, imitation, and generative learning. This study focuses on the last area: agents learn cooperative tactics from reward and reproduce expert maneuvers from a small set of demonstrations. Although developed for air combat, the methodology can be applied to other applications in aviation, such as commercial flight operations and pilot training.
